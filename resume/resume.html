Startup

Veya, founder (software and AI)veyaai.vercel.app

AI screening for retinopathy, glaucoma and cataract. An $80 smartphone attachment replaces a $15,000+ fundus camera, so clinics without an ophthalmologist can screen patients.

  • Trained an EfficientNet-B0 model on 12,000 labeled fundus images (IDRiD, ODIR-5K); 87% accuracy on a held-out, patient-split test set of 1,800 images
  • Designed the v0.4 attachment with co-founder: 11 3D-printed parts, 20D lens, 11 focus positions; risk report and PDF in about 15 seconds
  • Interviewed doctors; validated and tested the prototype (Sep 2026)
  • Preparing pilots with private clinics in Akmola Region (Q4 2026); first paid contracts planned for Q1 2027
  • Incubated by StartUp Orda (Astana Hub) and NIS Incubator
